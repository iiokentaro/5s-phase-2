AADT scale factors

A segment's AADT is its probe count (sample_size_avg) times the factor for its region: vehicles per day per probe.

The defaults come from the calibration in docs/AADT Scaling.xlsx: over the seven segments whose traffic was counted (four in Maharashtra, three in Thailand), the sum of the counted volumes over the sum of the probe counts. The result is held to 10–200,000 vehicles/day.
